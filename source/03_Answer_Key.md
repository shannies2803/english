## SECTION H — Conjunctions and Connectors

123. **Although he was tired, he continued working.** *(or: He was tired, but he continued working.)* 💡 *Do not use "although" and "but" together.*
124. **Because it rained, we stayed at home.** *(or: It rained, so we stayed at home.)*
125. because of 💡 *+ noun phrase.*
126. because 💡 *+ clause.*
127. Although 💡 *+ clause.*
128. Despite 💡 *+ noun.*
129. nor
130. but
131. or 💡 *= otherwise.*
132. however 💡 *Contrast — you would expect the match to stop.*
133. therefore 💡 *Result.*
134. Unless 💡 *"Unless" = if not.*

## SECTION L — Reported Speech

163. Ali said (that) he was hungry.
164. She said (that) she would help me the next day. 💡 *will → would; tomorrow → the next day.*
165. He asked where I lived. 💡 *Statement word order — not "where did I live".*
166. She asked whether/if I was coming.
167. The teacher told us to sit down.
168. He told us not to run in the corridor. 💡 *Negative command → "not to".*
169. Mei Ling said (that) she had finished her homework the day before.
170. She asked me to pass her the salt.
171. He suggested going swimming. *(also accept: He suggested that we should go swimming.)*
172. She exclaimed that it was a beautiful garden.
173. The teacher said (that) the earth is round. 💡 *No tense shift for a general truth.*
174. The boy said (that) he could swim.

## SECTION O — Synthesis and Transformation

193. **Although** he was tired, he continued working.
194. **Despite** being tired, he continued working. *(also: Despite his tiredness, …)*
195. The box was **too** heavy for Ali to carry.
196. The box was **so** heavy **that** Ali could not carry it.
197. It was **such an** interesting book **that** I read it twice.
198. **My wallet** was stolen yesterday.
199. **Grandmother said** (that) she would visit me the next day.
200. **Neither** Ali **nor** Siti attended the meeting.
201. She **not only** sings beautifully **but also** plays the piano.
202. **No sooner had** the bell rung **than** the pupils rushed out of the classroom. 💡 *Inversion + "than".*
203. **Unless** you apologise, she will not forgive you.
204. **It was not until** last week **that** he discovered the truth.
205. **By walking** to school, he saved money.
206. **Learning** a new language is not easy.
207. She woke up early **so that** she could catch the first bus.
208. The match was cancelled **because of** the heavy rain.
209. **The tourist asked** where the post office was.
210. She **prefers** tea **to** coffee.
211. **Rahim is** the tallest pupil in the class.
212. **He has** been working here since 2020.
213. **Although** my mother was angry, she did not scold me.
214. The pupils were **so** noisy **that** the teacher became angry.
215. **The man warned** us not to touch the wet paint.
216. The dog was **too** fierce for anybody to approach. *(also: … for anyone to dare to approach it.)*
217. **No other boy** in the class is as hardworking as he is. *(also: … is more hardworking than he is.)*
